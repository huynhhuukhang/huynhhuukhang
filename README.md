### 👨‍💻 Professional Profile

I am a **Systems Administrator** dedicated to architecting, deploying, and maintaining highly available IT infrastructure. Operating at the intersection of system administration and software engineering, I specialize in eliminating manual operational toil through **Python** and **Bash** automation.

Rather than just configuring servers, I treat infrastructure as a programmable entity. My homelab serves as a production-grade environment where I simulate enterprise scenarios, from deploying Type-1 hypervisors (Hyper-V) to managing complex networking routing, database provisioning, and automated disaster recovery pipelines.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Advanced RHEL/Ubuntu Administration, Network Security, and Backend Deployment.
- ⚙️ **Infrastructure Philosophy:** High Availability, Proactive Monitoring, and Zero-touch Provisioning.
- ⚡ **Recent Milestone:** Designed and deployed a full-stack Node.js/PostgreSQL application with automated database schemas.
